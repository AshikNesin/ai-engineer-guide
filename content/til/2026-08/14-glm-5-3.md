Z.ai team has released GLM-5.3 which performs as good as proprietary models like Fable, GPT-5.6 Sol, etc

And it is built on top of GLM-5.2

> Scaling post-training is all we did for GLM-5.3

![image.png](https://cdn.qblog.nesin.io/f_auto,q_auto/qblog/AIEngineerGuide/2026-08/camgsz2wzbiqdhyu3hdq)

## What's new?
> GLM-5.3 supports three thinking effort levels: low, high, and max. Disabling thinking is no longer supported by GLM-5.3.

![2026-08-14-at-23.50.422x.png](https://cdn.qblog.nesin.io/f_auto,q_auto/qblog/AIEngineerGuide/2026-08/dah3gekgxeqm6akwiqez)

It's available on GLM Coding Plan & ZCode as well.
