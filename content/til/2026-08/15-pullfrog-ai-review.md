[Pullfrog](https://pullfrog.com) is an open source alternative to CodeRabbit and other AI review apps.

![Screenshot-2026-08-16-at-01-01-56.png](https://cdn.qblog.nesin.io/f_auto,q_auto/qblog/AIEngineerGuide/2026-08/fpqfhyvilqmil0wrsgkf)

And it is free for personal use. You'll need to bring your own LLM though, rest of the things are taken care by them.

In my case, I've configured it with my GLM Coding Plan.

And they've really nice loading screen when the GitHub Actions are running
