Anomaly - the company behind OpenCode has released [Hex](https://anoma.ly/hex/) an [open source](https://anoma.ly/hex/) voice dictation app that runs entirely on your machine.

## How to get started?

```
brew install --cask anomalyco/tap/hex
```


And they've really nice interactive website. Click on that option button to play around with it.
