Addy Osmani from Anthropic has published a guide on which model for a task.

- `Opus 5.5 (medium)` which has better balance of everything and it is it recommended for coding and general.
- `Sonnet 5.5` when you know exactly what needs to be done with clear spec
- `Haiku 5.5` for routing, summarization and other minor tasks
- `Fable 5.2` for deep research and hard/complex coding task
