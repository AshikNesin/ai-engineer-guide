[Stageshow](https://benji.org/stageshow) skill helps you to build a good mock UI (faux) without looking like an AI slop.

It's by Benji Taylor who currently leads design at SpaceXAI and X.

The TLDR of what it does in a single sentense

> It reads as a real product, simplified to what the viewer needs, framed with intent, and every motion has a cause.


## How to get started?
```shell
npx skills add benjitaylor/skills --skill stageshow
```
