[Cursor](https://cursor.com) has launched a new plan for developers in India.

It's similar to ChatGPT Go. And come with ₹649 per month price range (pricing based on [PPP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purchasing_power_parity))

And you can pay via UPI. That's a good choice. Instead of expecting credit card which might be hard for students to get in India.

![2026-07-28-at-23.48.142x.png](https://cdn.qblog.nesin.io/f_auto,q_auto/qblog/AIEngineerGuide/2026-07/hganfz8vsbup14bnhg9s)

> Cursor Start sits between our two other individual plans, Free and Pro.

Note: You won't be able to subscribe to this plan if you've already have a subscription due to a [limitation](https://x.com/laurenleeplace/status/2082124295023833574) on their end. And recommends us to create a new account with a new email id instead.
