Anthropic is preparing to go IPO soon and it has filed it's [S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/2130357/000162828026067168/anthropicpbc-sx1.htm) which has important information about the business and how it is performing

It's going to get listed on Nasdaq with “ANT" symbol. Class A common stock. 

And other key numbers summary that I've got from Grok

| Category | Highlight | Details |
|----------|-----------|---------|
| **Revenue** | Explosive growth | $41M (2023) → $386M (2024) → $4.59B (2025) → $16.3B (H1 2026). Q2: $11.56B (+1,368% YoY) |
| **Profitability** | First operating profit | $974M in Q2 2026 |
| **Net loss** | Mostly non-cash | $80.7B H1 loss; ~$72.4B is paper charge on convertibles |
| **Cash** | Strong liquidity | ~$131B cash + short-term investments |
| **Compute** | Large locked-in deals | ~$450B+ non-cancelable cloud commitments (Google, AWS, Microsoft) |
| **Control** | Founders keep power | 50.1% voting rights forever; public shares capped at 49.9%; safety trust elects board majority |
| **Deals** | Partner investments | Amazon notes convert at $2.09 / $5.05; AMD buying up to $5B stock as GPUs deliver |
