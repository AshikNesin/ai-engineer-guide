[Upsy.ai](https://upsy.ai) by Upstash gives you a dedicated cloud computer with **browser, connectors, memory**, etc which you can wire up with any AI agents via MCP.

For now it's free. And under the hood uses `Box` feature by Upstash.

![Screenshot-2026-10-01-at-22-09-30.png](https://cdn.qblog.nesin.io/f_auto,q_auto/qblog/AIEngineerGuide/2026-10/k8u5v6fcl2h6hlio3x5e)

Once you connect it with your agent/assistant, it can interact with browser and other tools. Here is an example that I asked it to find a bus ticket pricing
