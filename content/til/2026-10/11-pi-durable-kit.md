If you're building agentic app using Swift for like iPhone/Mac app, you might find [PiDurableKit](https://github.com/finnvoor/PiDurableKit) interesting.

> Durable AI agents for iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Vision Pro, powered by @earendil-works/pi-durable running in JavaScriptCore.

> Conversations, model turns, tool calls, and your own state are committed to storage before anything is shown. If iOS kills your app mid-turn, reopening the storage picks the work up where it stopped.

Under the hood, they're using JavaScriptCore to run pi-durable.
