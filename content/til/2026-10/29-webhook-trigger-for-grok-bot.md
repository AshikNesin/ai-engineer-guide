Sometime you might want to send information to your Grok bot from an external system.

Say, a customer has rised a issue and you want to do let the Grok bot do the initial analysis or something.

For such cases, you can use the **webhooks** trigger for your Grok bot.

## How to get started?
Just tell your bot, to create a routine with webhook trigger and it'll create a new routine for you.

![1000656996.png](https://cdn.qblog.nesin.io/f_auto,q_auto/qblog/AIEngineerGuide/2026-10/mnz15m40tkpwrvfmihha)

And from the created webhook, you can get the credentials needed to trigger the webhooks.

![1000656997.png](https://cdn.qblog.nesin.io/f_auto,q_auto/qblog/AIEngineerGuide/2026-10/cmr0kdsw5pzxcowvhevz)
