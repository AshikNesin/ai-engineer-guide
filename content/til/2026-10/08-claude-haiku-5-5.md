Anthropic has released their latest version of their Haiku model - **Claude Haiku 5.5**

And it costs as cheap as OpenAI's Luna model. (75% less than Haiku 4.5)

> Haiku 5.5 is especially good value on tasks under 100,000 tokens, which make up around 90% of requests to our previous Haiku model.
