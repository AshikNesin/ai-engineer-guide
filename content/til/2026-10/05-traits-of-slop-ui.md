Came across this blog post on UI slop.

If you look around, you'll also be seeing these things everywhere.

Common traits are:
- Gradients
- Too many random colors
- Badges - even if it doesn't make sense
- Misalignment - I see that even in apps like Youtube sometimes :(
- Too many emojis
- Generic taglines

You can read more about it here:
https://godobject.dev/blog/10-tells-of-slop/
