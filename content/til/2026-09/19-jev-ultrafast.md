jev-ultrafast is an agentic browser which under the hood uses Jev model to figure out the action.

And only if it is needed for TYPE_TEXT operation it uses the LLM.

Rest of the things are handled by Typesafe.ai's Jev model

In the demo video, they were able to book flights using it in less than 7.1 seconds from Zürich → London on Google Flights

https://github.com/browser-use/jev-ultrafast
